🎙️ Captain Dad's Recording Studio

How it works

  1. Press ● Record, read the words on the card, then press ■ Stop. Press ▶ Play to check it, and record again if you want another go.
  2. Your recordings are saved in this browser as you go, so you can do a few at a time and come back later. Use the same browser on the same computer.
  3. When you're done, press Download all. You'll get a zip with an audio folder inside.
  4. Unzip it and put the audio folder in your GitHub repo, next to index.html. Commit and push, and Vercel updates the game.
  5. In the game, choose Captain Dad on the first page. Any page you haven't recorded yet uses the computer voice instead.
  • Quiet room, mic about a hand's width away, and leave a second of silence at the start and end.
  • Sonny is the pirate in blue and Wylie is the pirate in orange. Read the choices at the bottom of each card if you like, or skip them.
  • Recorded somewhere else, like your phone? Use ⬆ Upload on that card instead.
