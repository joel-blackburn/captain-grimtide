🎙️ Captain Dad's Recording Studio

The Curse of Captain Grimtide, 3D version · one card per line

How it works

  1. Press ● Record, read the words on the card, then press ■ Stop. Press ▶ Play to check it, and record again if you want another go.
  2. Your recordings are saved in this browser as you go, so you can do a few at a time and come back later. Use the same browser on the same computer.
  3. When you're done, press Download all. You'll get a zip with an audio3d folder inside.
  4. Unzip it and send me the audio3d folder (or put it in the GitHub repo next to beta.html). Vercel updates the game.
  5. In the game, choose Captain Dad as the reader. Any line you haven't recorded yet uses the computer voice instead, so you can record bit by bit.
  • Quiet room, mic about a hand's width away, and leave a second of silence at the start and end.
  • You can read every part, or pick one voice at a time with the Show filter. Lines that say the same thing in two places only need recording once.
  • Mum's "BREAKFAAAST!" is Koby's card.
  • Recorded somewhere else, like your phone? Use ⬆ Upload on that card instead.
  • Faded cards change every game (like counting pieces of eight), so the computer voice always reads those.
